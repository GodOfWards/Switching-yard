# Time

The clock underneath is #70.

## Rules

- **The clock runs at real time.** Shifts are 8 hours: 06–14, 14–22,
  22–06. A new yard starts at 06:00, day 1.
- **Skip to an event:** a Skip button lists the next few events (a job
  falls due, a road train arrives or leaves, a shift change) and jumps to the one tapped.
- **Skip to a time:** a chosen clock time on a 10-minute mark (xx:x0).
- **Only when everything the player could touch stands still:** their loco
  stopped, no cars rolling. No physics runs during a skip.
- **Jobs not taken that fall due during a skip are done off-screen**, and
  the skip warns first which jobs it hands to other crews.
