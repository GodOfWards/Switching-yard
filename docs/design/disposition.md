# Disposition

What should happen next to a car or block, here, once it has arrived or
its state has changed. Reception, inspection, classification and train
formation are related but distinct processes.

## Rules

- **A car or block may:** continue together; remain as a block; be
  classified; go to an industry; go to storage; go for repair; be
  incorporated into another train; or simply remain where it is.
- **Not moving a car can be the correct decision.**
- **The game decides dispositions**, as a yardmaster's instructions; the
  player carries them out. The player may take over the yardmaster's role
  later.
- **Cars aren't sent straight to their ultimate destination track** on
  arrival: the disposition decides the next step only.

- **Placeholder rule until the research is in** (#45): a car's waybill
  destination sends it to its block track; a bad-order car goes to the
  repair track; a car with no new waybill stays where it is.
