# Living railway

The yard should feel like part of a living railway, not an isolated
switching puzzle. All of this is long-term.

## Rules

- **Other trains don't need to be jobs.** Their movements can simply
  constrain the player's work. Example: the player is switching near the
  main line, a train approaches, the main must be cleared, the player
  holds inside yard limits, the train passes, work resumes. That gives
  "can I finish this move before the train arrives?"
- **Work the player doesn't take is done off-screen** for now; other crews
  doing it visibly on the map is backlog.

- **The railway beyond the yard is a line graph**: stations, junctions,
  distances and shape, made from OpenStreetMap
  ([rail-network-junin.md](../reference/rail-network-junin.md)). Full
  switching track exists only in yards that are played. "Active" track
  means track in the FA era, 1977 or later: in use or disused, not
  lifted, and not closed before the game's time.

## The main line

Grounded in FA's rulebook
([fa-yard-operations.md](../reference/fa-yard-operations.md), Authority to
move).

- **The main is off limits unless authorized.** Authority is needed for
  the running tracks only: the main and the crossover joining it to the
  head-shunt. The yard's tracks are free; the player is the yard crew,
  under the station's standing consent.
- **The crossover is padlocked, lying for the main.** The player asks the
  station for the main for a time (10, 20 or 30 minutes). The station
  grants it, unlocking the crossover, when no train is due through in
  that time; otherwise it refuses and says when the next train is due.
- **Each authority is one use.** When done, the player gets off the
  running tracks, sets the crossover for the main and reports clear; the
  station padlocks it again. Kept past the time asked for is noted on the
  results of the job being worked.
- **Through freights** pass on the main without working the yard, two
  each way a day, as real trains the game drives. Each is offered 10
  minutes before it reaches the map. If the main is clear, it runs
  through.
- **A train finding the main not clear is held**: it stands at the east
  home signal, or waits at the station side, until the player reports
  clear. A train offered while the player holds the main calls them off.
  The wait is the player's delay, noted on the results of the job being
  worked. No wreck is possible: the train obeys the signal.
- **The *Aconcagua*** (#77), a passenger train, runs through once each
  way a day like the freights: 565 from Retiro at 15:30 from the east, 566
  from Mendoza at 23:30 from the west.
- **A passenger train gets no conditional line clear at night** (#104).
  By day it is handled like a freight. Between 20:00 and 06:00, if the
  main isn't clear when it is offered, line clear is refused: it waits at
  the station behind (La Oriental from the east, Junín station from the
  west), out of sight, and the main chip says so. Once the player reports
  clear it is offered again and sets off, reaching the map 10 minutes
  later. Its delay, noted like any held train's, is its lateness at the
  map's edge, so a refusal costs at least the 10-minute run-in more than
  a conditional line clear would have.

## Retiro road trains

Retiro's road trains (#78) run in from the east over the crossover, and
out the same way, so every one crosses the head-shunt and the ladder, the
player's working space. Mendoza's still come and go off the map at the
station side ([traffic.md](traffic.md)).

- **The routes.** In: the main from the east edge, the crossover, the
  lead, the ladder, Track 3 to the buffer, loco first. Out: Track 1, the
  ladder, the lead, the crossover, the main to the east edge.
- **An arrival is offered** 10 minutes before it reaches the map, about
  15 minutes before it is due on Track 3: a notice and the main chip.
- **It is accepted only** with Track 3 clear, its route clear of vehicles
  and fouling, the main not held by the player, and no through train due
  on the main before it is in clear (#57's grant rule). Otherwise it is
  **refused** and waits at La Oriental, off the map, and is offered again
  once all of that holds, still 10 minutes out. A player holding the main
  is called off, as for a through train.
- **On acceptance the station sets and locks the route**: the crossover
  and the ladder switches on it, shown padlocked, until the train is in
  clear. The player only keeps the route clear.
- **It runs in on sight**, driven by the game: line speed on the main,
  15 km/h from the crossover on, stopping short of anything put on its
  route. No wreck. Any such stop is the yard's delay.
- **At the buffer the road loco cuts off** and goes on west, off the map,
  toward the station. The cars stand coupled and secured, and the
  break-up job opens. The station unlocks the ladder switches and
  padlocks the crossover again.
- **A departure's road loco comes off the depot fan**, light, 20 minutes
  before departure, once the build is ready (reported done, or done by
  another crew if not taken). Its route, fan to Track 7, reversing to the
  lead past J1, reversing again to Track 1, needs no main; it is accepted
  and locked as an arrival's. It couples to the east end of Track 1's cut
  and the road crew hooks up, taking the cars for the train from that end.
- **The station checks the train** for 10 minutes, standing: brake test
  and paperwork (RITO art. 207).
- **At its time it pulls out**, accepted as an arrival is, over the
  crossover, and leaves at the east edge.
- **Lateness goes in the register** with the yard's cause: "Refused:
  Track 3 not clear", "Route not clear", "Build not ready", "Main held".
  Waiting on a booked through train, or on another road train, is not
  the yard's delay, **unless the departure, leaving at its booked time,
  would have been accepted ahead of that through train** (#47): then the
  whole wait but on other road trains is the yard's, "Lost its path behind
  2401, build not ready" (or "route not clear", "main held").
- **A skip** does a road train's moves at once, at their times: an
  arrival accepted is laid on Track 3 as before #78, a departure's cars
  leave at its time. A skip waits while one is moving.
