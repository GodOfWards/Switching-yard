# Living railway

The yard should feel like part of a living railway, not an isolated
switching puzzle. All of this is long-term.

## Rules

- **Other trains don't need to be jobs.** Their movements can simply
  constrain the player's work. Example: the player is switching near the
  main line, a train approaches, the main must be cleared, the player
  holds inside yard limits, the train passes, work resumes. That gives
  "can I finish this move before the train arrives?"
- **Work the player doesn't take is done off-screen** for now; other crews
  doing it visibly on the map is backlog.

- **The railway beyond the yard is a line graph**: stations, junctions,
  distances and shape, made from OpenStreetMap
  ([rail-network-junin.md](../reference/rail-network-junin.md)). Full
  switching track exists only in yards that are played. "Active" track
  means track in the FA era, 1977 or later: in use or disused, not
  lifted, and not closed before the game's time.

## The main line

Grounded in FA's rulebook
([fa-yard-operations.md](../reference/fa-yard-operations.md), Authority to
move).

- **The main is off limits unless authorized.** Authority is needed for
  the running tracks only: the main and the crossover joining it to the
  head-shunt. The yard's tracks are free; the player is the yard crew,
  under the station's standing consent.
- **The crossover is padlocked, lying for the main.** The player asks the
  station for the main for a time (10, 20 or 30 minutes). The station
  grants it, unlocking the crossover, when no train is due through in
  that time; otherwise it refuses and says when the next train is due.
- **Each authority is one use.** When done, the player gets off the
  running tracks, sets the crossover for the main and reports clear; the
  station padlocks it again. Kept past the time asked for is noted on the
  results of the job being worked.
- **Through freights** pass on the main without working the yard, two
  each way a day, as real trains the game drives. Each is offered 10
  minutes before it reaches the map. If the main is clear, it runs
  through.
- **A train finding the main not clear is held**: it stands at the east
  home signal, or waits at the station side, until the player reports
  clear. A train offered while the player holds the main calls them off.
  The wait is the player's delay, noted on the results of the job being
  worked. No wreck is possible: the train obeys the signal.
