# Living railway

The yard should feel like part of a living railway, not an isolated
switching puzzle. All of this is long-term.

## Rules

- **Other trains don't need to be jobs.** Their movements can simply
  constrain the player's work. Example: the player is switching near the
  main line, a train approaches, the main must be cleared, the player
  holds inside yard limits, the train passes, work resumes. That gives
  "can I finish this move before the train arrives?"
- **Work the player doesn't take is done off-screen** for now; other crews
  doing it visibly on the map is backlog.

- **The railway beyond the yard is a line graph**: stations, junctions,
  distances and shape, made from OpenStreetMap
  ([rail-network-junin.md](../reference/rail-network-junin.md)). Full
  switching track exists only in yards that are played. "Active" track
  means track in the FA era, 1977 or later: in use or disused, not
  lifted, and not closed before the game's time.
